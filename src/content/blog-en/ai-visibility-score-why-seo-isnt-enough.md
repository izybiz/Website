The average AI visibility score sits at 38 out of 100. That's the finding from a 2026 Surfeo study of 518 companies, tested against ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude. **91% of them stay below 60 points: AI models barely ever mention them** (Source: [Surfeo](https://surfeo.ai/en/blog/ai-visibility-report-2026), 2026). A solid Google ranking says nothing about that number.

## Does ranking well on Google guarantee a ChatGPT mention?

No. The same report flags a separate finding: only 10% of top-ranking Google sites also show up in ChatGPT's answers. Ranking measures a position in a list of links. Getting cited by an AI means something else. It means getting named, with facts that hold up, inside an answer someone else wrote. **A page that ranks well on Google, with no brand mentions anywhere else, stays invisible to AI.**

## What's the difference between an SEO score and an AI visibility score?

An SEO score measures a position. An AI visibility score measures a mention.

| Criterion | SEO score | AI visibility score |
|---|---|---|
| What it measures | A page's position in search results | A mention of the company in a generated answer |
| Playing field | Google | ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude |
| What matters most | Keywords, backlinks, technical setup | Brand mentions, original data, customer reviews |
| What the buyer sees | A list of links to compare | Two or three names already recommended |
| Warning sign on a drop | Visible in a rank-tracking tool | None, until someone actually asks the AI |

**The two scores can drift apart. No tracking tool will flag it.**

## Why are service businesses so poorly represented in AI answers?

Because nothing in their online presence gives the AI anything to name them by. **56.6% of audited companies score below 40 points. That's the line under which AI barely ever mentions them. Only four score above 80** (Source: Surfeo, 2026). A score that low isn't rare. It's the norm for the sector. The pattern holds regardless of size. A low score isn't a sign of being small or new. It's what happens when a business never shows up anywhere an AI would look.

## How do I know if my company gets cited by AI?

The test takes ten minutes. Ask ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity the five questions a real buyer would ask. Note who gets mentioned.

Picture an industrial maintenance company. It's ranked well on Google for two years, in its own region. The owner asks ChatGPT the same question. The answer names three rivals, two of them smaller. **No tracking tool would have caught it.**

## What should I do to improve my AI visibility score?

Publishing your own numbers matters most. Surfeo (2026) points to average turnaround times, renewal rates, volumes handled, not generic claims copied from a rival's site. A company's name also needs to show up beyond its own website. Trade directories, local press, trade groups: all of it counts. An AI can't cite what it can't find anywhere else. One detail few businesses watch: Gemini filters out companies rated below 3.5 stars. That makes reviews a citation lever, not just a reputation one.

Classic SEO stays the foundation. **AI visibility adds to it. It doesn't replace it.**
