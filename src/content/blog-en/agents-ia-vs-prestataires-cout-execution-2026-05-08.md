**€600/day for a freelance AI consultant. A few euros for the same mission handled by AI agents.**

That's not the right question to ask. The real comparison isn't the daily rate — it's what you're actually buying.

---

Articles about the "cost of AI agents" are talking about something else: deploying a custom AI project runs between €5,000 and €80,000 according to industry analyses (Gartner, McKinsey, 2024). That's a transformation budget. Not the cost of an executed mission.

The question that matters to a manager is different. You already have recurring tasks you hand off to service providers — market monitoring, reporting, content production, coordination. For those tasks, what changes if AI agents execute them instead?

## What does a recurring-execution provider really cost?

€600 to €900/day for an experienced AI consultant (source: plateya.fr, bgbformation.fr) — before the mission even starts. Sourcing delays, contractual rigidity, deliverables with no traceability: these are costs the invoice never shows. Sometimes two to six weeks before it even begins. A fixed scope — if the scope changes, so does the invoice.

## What's the real cost of an AI agent in business for a recurring mission?

Evaluating the cost of an AI agent in business starts with changing the unit of measure: not the upfront investment, but the cost per mission executed each month.

A monthly subscription. For the price of half a day of a service provider, you get a month of execution. No surprise invoice if you restart the mission. The mission starts the day you brief it, in plain language. Not in six weeks. Not after onboarding. Day one.

That's the model Izybiz has chosen — a SaaS platform of AI agents, not a consulting firm or a service provider. You describe the mission in plain language, the agents organize themselves, and you approve the steps that matter.

But the most underrated variable isn't cost. It's control.

With a service provider, you control the milestones. Not the execution. With AI agents, you approve the steps that matter. Mission assigned on Monday, deliverable on Wednesday, two checkpoints — execution is tracked, not a black box. The final call stays with you.

## For which tasks is the AI agent cost unbeatable?

Market monitoring and synthesis. Recurring reporting. Content production. Coordination and process tracking. Daily or weekly tasks — the ones that take up the most of your teams' time and don't fit well into a fixed contract.

Where service providers remain irreplaceable: strategic advice, rare industry expertise, and the human relationship in moments that matter.

If you've already tried AI tools without results, you were probably right to stop. Those tools require work to run — you were the operator, not the beneficiary. The difference here: you describe the mission, you don't build the automation yourself.

Sticking with your current providers is a decision. It has a price — not just in euros, but in execution speed and visibility into what's actually been produced. The question is whether that's the price you want to pay.

## A concrete example: delegating weekly market monitoring

A sales director at an industrial mid-sized company (150 employees) used to spend three hours a week compiling market monitoring on competitors and target markets himself. The mission was handed to AI agents: a weekly synthesis from 5 sources, formatted to be shared with the executive committee.

Result after 30 days: 3 hours reclaimed per week, a structured deliverable landing every Monday at 7am, better-documented executive decisions. Monthly cost: less than half a day of consulting.

This type of mission — recurring, structurable, with no human added value in the execution itself — is exactly where an AI agent's cost is unbeatable.
