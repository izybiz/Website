You probably still use Google to search for an AI agent platform. That's normal. But the first impression is now formed somewhere else — before the Google search even begins.

**In four months, the number of managers who open ChatGPT first has grown by 71% — and 44% of platforms don't appear in those answers** (Source: G2 / DerivateX, 2026).

---

## Why does ranking on Google's first page no longer guarantee you exist in ChatGPT?

You may have typed "best AI agent platform" into ChatGPT last week without really thinking about it. Or you're about to, in the coming weeks.

A manager asks the question directly: "What's the best platform for delegating tasks to a team of AI agents?" They read the answer. They form a first impression. If a platform isn't cited, it doesn't exist for them.

**What's counterintuitive: a recent startup well documented by third parties outperforms a market leader with a 30% share in Perplexity's answers** (Source: DerivateX, 2026). It's not brand recognition that determines AI visibility. It's distribution.

Being in Google's top 10 no longer guarantees being cited in AI answers. In mid-2025, 75% of platforms cited in AI Overviews came from the top 10. By early 2026, that rate had fallen to between 17% and 38% (Source: Celestia Studio, 2026).

46.7% of the sources cited by Perplexity come from Reddit (Source: Discovered Labs, 2025). Not corporate blogs. Real conversations between practitioners.

If a platform is absent from these discussions, you won't see it. You won't end up choosing a worse one by bad luck — you'll choose a different one, convinced you searched thoroughly.

---

## How can you test your visibility in AI answers?

Test your next tool search in ChatGPT before Google — the two lists are different.

Compare the platforms cited by ChatGPT against the ones ranking on Google — two distinct rankings.

Ask a candidate platform: do you appear in ChatGPT for "AI agents for SME managers"? No answer is itself a signal — it means there's no content strategy behind it.
